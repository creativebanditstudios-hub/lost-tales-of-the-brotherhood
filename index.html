Lost Tales of the Brotherhood

A 3D-animated Star Wars Legends fan film · ~3 minutes

About the film

In a dark Sith training temple, Ashura Isradia Sadow — a character of the Dark Jedi Brotherhood, Clan Naga Sadow — stands in dark-and-gold hooded masked armour with dual red lightsabers. He battles training droids across a glowing red-orange rune circle, then duels and defeats The Apprentice, a pale blonde woman in black robes, ending with Force lightning.

“You have a long way to go.”

Made with Unreal Engine 5 (MetaHuman, Movie Render Queue) and Blender (Sith training temple).
